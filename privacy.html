Comet Loop: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Comet Loop ("the app") is a single-player offline arcade game published by Cleared Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or device information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no internet access.

Information stored on your device

Your best score, banked sparks, unlocked skins, and sound setting are saved locally on your device so your progress is kept between sessions. This data never leaves your device unless Android's own device backup is enabled, in which case it is handled by Google under your Google account settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this data.

Sharing your score

If you tap "Share", Android opens its standard share sheet with a text message containing your score. You choose whether and where to send it. The app does not see or store what happens after that.

Rating the app

The app may occasionally show Google Play’s standard in-app rating prompt. That prompt is provided and operated by Google Play, and any rating or review you submit is handled by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. The app does not receive your rating, review, or account details.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
